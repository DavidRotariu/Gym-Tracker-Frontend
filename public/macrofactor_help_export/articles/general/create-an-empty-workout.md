The empty workout feature lets you create a quick, one-off workout. It’s useful if you want to train outside a program, try new exercises, or log a single session.

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Under **More**, tap **Empty Workout**.
3. Tap the **+** button > **Add Exercises**.
4. After adding all exercises to your workout, tap **Done**.
5. At the top of the screen, review the following information:

   * **Target muscles:** Displays the muscle groups targeted by the exercises you add.
   * **Total exercises**: Shows the total number of exercises in the workout.
6. When you are ready, tap **Start Workout** or **Save Workout Plan** at the bottom of the screen.

### Gym selection

To update your gym profile for your empty workout, tap your gym profile icon at the top of the screen next to the three-dot setting icon. You can also add a new gym profile by tapping **Manage** **Gym Profiles** > **Add New Gym Profile** > **Save**.

### Workout options menu

Tap the three-dot icon in the top-right corner to access additional options:

* **Swap gym for workout:** Change the gym profile used for the workout.
* **Reset workout:** Clear time, sets, weights, and any other changes made to the workout.
* **Export workout:** Save an .xlsx file for the workout.
* **Archive Workout:** Moves your workout to your archive.
* **Delete Workout:** Deletes your workout.
