Here, you can ensure your Dashboard displays the data you want to see first when you open the app.

![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/X7vmwUWBGTOenDs9nbwLEGcgwovmmES9MA6tr5BZe4EDmAbI6nT9P3WpwabNZxn3LyGyrFMf18y.png)

**To Customize Your Dashboard**

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. Under **Feature Settings**, tap **Dashboard**.
3. Use the up and down arrows to move tiles to your preferred location.
4. Tap **Save** in the top-right corner to save your changes.

**Add or Remove Widgets**

1. In the **Customize Dashboard** menu, tap **Add or** **Remove Insights** under each section.
2. Toggle on or off the details you want displayed in your dashboard tiles.
3. Tap **X** or **Done** to go back
4. Tap **Save** in the top-right corner to save your changes.

You can also swipe left on a widget and tap the remove icon to remove it. To return to the original layout, tap **Reset to Original App Defaults** at the bottom of the screen.
