**For a workout in a program:**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Locate the program that contains the workout you want to begin.
3. **Tap the arrow** to expand the program block if it is collapsed.

   ![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/w5AEfL8gDBoJXt1NPV2P0eB3xPwEmxMmbQpZDgfmiCelROCoiEDjaNq4456YcVqH0LzKErXGBY8.png)
4. Tap the workout you want to begin. You will see a preview showing target muscles, total exercises, estimated duration, and rep details.
5. Tap **Start Workout** at the bottom of the screen to begin the session.

![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/hyf0eiAYcCjqgXHilkKKuRbPussfH4ZDw4YCxfUY12R5ggQDYBgwF2xvlsGN5eSZ9RLaasikYHj.png)

**For a single workout:**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Locate the workout you want to begin in your workout library. 

   ![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/U02B4ENGgTi0BkIQJYaUhDBolFoXgt3CdHKfOgR1nOVKznqnnOuWgxLrMIamI12v2WMsL1rNTr6.png)
3. Tap the workout you want to begin. You will see a preview showing target muscles, total exercises, estimated duration, and rep details.
4. Tap **Start Workout** at the bottom of the screen to begin the session.

   ![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/3ERicDvFYRKfvqTiyWh0olrrxgyOZdmaxZcr2ltIYsKKlx8V2nyojGwESGX1GN63UZsie4CugIi.png)
