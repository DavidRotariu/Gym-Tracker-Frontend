The exercises section focuses on how individual lifts are progressing over time. This is useful for identifying performance trends within specific exercises rather than just across workouts or muscle groups.

**Viewing an Exercise**

1. Go to the **Dashboard** tab.
2. Scroll down to **Exercises**.
3. Tap **See All**.
4. Select an exercise.

Available views include:

**1-RM, 3-RM, and 10-RM:** Estimates changes in estimated strength at different rep ranges over time.  
**Total Volume:** The total amount of weight for that exercise.  
**Best Set Volume:** Your highest single-set volume recorded for that exercise.  
**Heaviest Weight**: The maximum load you have lifted for that exercise.  
**Total Reps:** The total number of reps completed for that exercise over time.  
**Best Set Reps**: The highest number of reps you’ve recorded in a single set.  
**Total Sets:** All sets you have performed for that exercise to date.  
  
For each view, you can adjust the time range to short or long-term trends.
