section: "General"

---

If you are brand new and have not logged any workouts yet, MacroFactor Workouts does not provide weight recommendations. You will need to choose your own starting weight for each exercise.

A good starting point is a weight that allows you to complete the target reps while matching the suggested [RIR](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/385-what-is-rir-and-how-should-i-use-it-during-training) for that set. The exact weight does not need to be perfect, since the app uses your logged sets to learn from your performance. Just start with a conservative weight, and gradually increase the load until the set feels appropriately challenging.

Once you begin logging workouts, MacroFactor Workouts uses the weights, reps, and RIR you log [to adjust recommendations](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/305-understanding-and-using-smart-progressions). As more data is collected, your recommendations will become more personalized and updated based on your actual performance.
