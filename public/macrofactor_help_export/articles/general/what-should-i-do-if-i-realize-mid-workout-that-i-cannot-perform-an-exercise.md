If a piece of equipment is unavailable or an exercise does not feel right that day, you can [swap the exercise](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/312-swap-exercises) for another option.

**Option 1**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Open any program or workout you have created.
3. Scroll to the exercise you want to replace.
4. Tap the three dots on the right side of the exercise, tap **Swap**.
5. Review the smart substitutions shown by the app.
6. Select the exercise you want and tap **Replace**.

If the exercise you would like to replace is not listed under Smart Substitutions, continue with the directions below:

7. If you want a different option, tap **Find Other Replacements** at the bottom.
8. Search for the exercise you want to use instead.
9. Tap the **+** button next to the exercise.
10. Tap **Done** to confirm the swap.

**Option 2**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Start a workout.
3. Tap the exercise you want to swap from the top navigation bar.
4. In the action bar, under the exercise title, tap **Swap**.
5. Review the smart substitutions shown by the app.
6. Select the exercise you want and tap **Replace**.

If the exercise you would like to replace is not listed under Smart Substitutions, continue with the directions below:

7. If you want a different option, tap **Find Other Replacements** at the bottom.
8. Search for the exercise you want to use instead.
9. Tap the **+** button next to the exercise.
10. Tap **Done** to confirm the swap.

The new exercise replaces the original one. Note, the progression for the new movement is based on the sets you log going forward.
