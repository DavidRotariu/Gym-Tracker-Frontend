If you want to change your selections, such as removing body metrics you no longer want to track or adding new ones, you can do so at any time.

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. Tap **Data Visibility**, under **Data Management**.
3. Tap **Body Metrics Visibility**.
4. Toggle the progress photos and body metrics you want to track.
5. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your selections.

If you’d like tips on taking good progress photos or high-quality body measurements, you’ll likely find the knowledge base entries linked above helpful.
