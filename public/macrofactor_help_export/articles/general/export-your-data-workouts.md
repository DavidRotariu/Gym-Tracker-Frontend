The best way to export your data in bulk is to use the Data Export feature.

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. Under **Data Management**, tap **Data Export**.
3. Once you’re there, you have two options to choose from: **Quick Export** or **Granular Export**.

### Quick Export

If you choose Quick Export, you can:

* Select a time frame for the export.
* Choose whether to include MacroFactor Workouts data, MacroFactor Nutrition data, or both.

Quick Export generates a single spreadsheet that provides a high-level snapshot of your selected data over the chosen time period.

### Granular Export

If you choose Granular Export, you can select specific datasets to export in more detail.

This includes individual workout-related data such as:

* Exercises
* Muscle groups
* Gym profiles

You can also include nutrition and weight data, if desired.

After making your selections, tap **Export** to generate the spreadsheet.
