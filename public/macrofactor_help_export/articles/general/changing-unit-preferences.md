If you want to use kilograms instead of pounds, feet and inches instead of centimeters, or adjust time units, you can update those settings here.

To access unit preferences:

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. Under **General**, tap **Units**.
3. Adjust your unit preferences as needed.
