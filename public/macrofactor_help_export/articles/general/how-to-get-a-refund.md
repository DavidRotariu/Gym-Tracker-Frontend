All of our payments are handled via the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store. The process varies depending on your platform.

### For the Apple App Store:

We are unable to cancel/manage subscriptions or issue refunds from our side. The trials, subscriptions, and payment processing are all securely handled by Apple. This is true of all apps that exclusively use Apple's payment system.

To request a refund, you will need to use [Apple's support portal](https://getsupport.apple.com/).  
  
From there, tap **iPhone**, then **Subscriptions and purchases**, then **Request a refund**.

In general, Apple processes refunds within 60 days of payment, unless there are issues with the transaction.

### For the Google Play Store:

If you contact the Google Play Store directly, they will process a refund so long as it’s within 48 hours of the purchase.

We are also able to offer refunds from our end. Contact us in the app by going to **More** > Community & Support > **Support** > **Talk with MF Support**, or shoot us an email at [support@macrofactorapp.com](mailto:support@macrofactorapp.com), and we can handle it for you.

Because our trials, subscriptions, and payments are handled directly by Google Play, it may take some time between when we issue the refund and when Google Play processes the refund, but you should receive a confirmation email from Google Play shortly after the refund is processed.
