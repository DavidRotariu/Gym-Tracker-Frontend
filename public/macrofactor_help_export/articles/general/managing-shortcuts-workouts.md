Shortcuts let you get to the parts of the app you use most without digging through menus. To get there, you have two options.

**Option 1**

1. Tap the **+** icon in the center of the bottom navigation to open **Shortcuts**.
2. To the right of **Shortcuts**, tap the **settings icon** to open shortcut settings.

**Option 2**

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. Under **Feature Settings**, tap **Shortcuts**.
3. From the settings screen, you can control how shortcuts are displayed.

**Available workout shortcuts**

* **Weight:** Quickly log a weigh-in.
* **Metrics:** Add body-fat percentage or body measurements.
* **Photos:** Add progress photos.
* **History:** Jump to your workout history.
* **New program:** Start building a new training program.
* **New workout:** Create a one-off workout.

Tap **Add or Remove Shortcuts** to toggle which shortcuts appear.

### Ordering shortcuts

With **Order** selected, drag the handle on the right to change the order shortcuts appear in the app. The first item will show at the top.

### Color options

With **Color** selected, you can assign a color to each shortcut. Colors are optional and only affect how shortcuts look, not how they function.

### Resetting changes

Tap **Reset** **to App Defaults** to restore the original shortcut order and colors.
