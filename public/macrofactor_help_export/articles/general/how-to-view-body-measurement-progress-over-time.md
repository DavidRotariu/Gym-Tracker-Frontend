You can visualize changes to any of your tracked body measurements over time.

1. Go to the Dashboard tab.
2. Under **Body Metrics**, tap **See All**. Here, you can view the measurements you have selected to display on the inner dashboard.
3. To view metrics for specific days, tap **Full Body**, then tap **View All Metrics**.
4. Use the horizontal tab bar at the top of the screen to quickly switch between predefined date ranges.

### Comparing your Body Measurement Progress

The **Compare All Metrics** screen lets you view changes in measurements such as body-fat percentage, body weight, and specific body parts, such as the waist or shoulders.

**To compare metrics:**

1. Open the **Compare All Metrics** screen.
2. Use the horizontal tab bar at the top to switch between predefined date ranges.
3. To select custom dates, tap the **calendar icon** in the top-right corner.
4. Review visual body fat, which is displayed as a percentage change.
5. Compare individual body measurements shown side-by-side to see progress over time.
