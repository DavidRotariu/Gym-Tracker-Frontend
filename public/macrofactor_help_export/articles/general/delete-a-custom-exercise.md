title: "Delete a Custom Exercise"
section: "General"
source_url: https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/330-delete-a-custom-exercise
---

1. Go to **More** > Feature Settings > **Exercises**.
2. Use the top navigation to select **Custom**.

You will see a list of custom exercises you have created. Use the search bar at the bottom if needed.

3. Tap the exercise you want to delete.
4. Scroll to the bottom of the screen and tap **Delete Exercise**.
