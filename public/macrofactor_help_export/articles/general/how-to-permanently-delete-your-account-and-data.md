You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time. This deletion is instant and cannot be undone. After signing in to the app, you can delete your account and data whether or not you have an active subscription.

You also have options for removing data without deleting your account. Deletion can be done in one of two ways:

### **Granular delete**

Delete specific data types without removing your account. Granular delete lets you permanently remove individual categories of data.

### **Bulk delete**

Bulk delete lets you remove multiple categories of data at once while keeping your account active. This action cannot be undone.

**If your subscription is active**

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. Under **Data Management**, tap **Account & Data Deletion**.
3. Toggle to **Delete Your MacroFactor Account and All Associated Data.**
4. Tap **Delete Data** at the bottom of your screen to delete your account.

**If your subscription is inactive**

1. Tap the three-dot icon in the top-right corner of the screen.
2. Tap **Delete Account & Data**.

**Important:** Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. To cancel or manage your subscription, you must do so through your phone’s app store.
