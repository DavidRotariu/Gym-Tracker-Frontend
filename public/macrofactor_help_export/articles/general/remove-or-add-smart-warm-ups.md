Smart warm-ups help improve performance by automatically calculating warm-up weights and reps for your session. They also help ease you into heavier sets without overdoing it. The app does this by scaling warm-up sets based on the exercise and your current working loads.

Warm-ups are automatically added to each exercise in your workouts based on the selected scheme. **By default, smart warm-ups are enabled.**

**Option 1:**

1. Go to **More** > Feature Settings > **Workouts.**
2. Scroll down to **Add Smart Warm-Ups** and toggle it on or off depending your preference.

**Option 2:**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Start a workout.
3. Tap the exercise you want to add a warm-up to from the top navigation bar.
4. In the action bar, under the exercise title, tap **Warm Up**.
5. This will instantly add a warm-up set.
6. Swipe left on the warm-up set and tap the red button to remove a warm-up set.

**Option 3:**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Start a workout.
3. Tap the three horizontal lines in the top-left corner of the screen.
4. Select **Workout Settings**.
5. Under **Warm-Up**, toggle **Add Smart Warm-Ups** on or off.
