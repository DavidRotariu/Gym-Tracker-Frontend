It’s possible that during your workout you may want to pause your session or minimize it to do other activities within the app or on your phone.

### To pause or minimize a workout

1. While in an active workout, tap the three horizontal lines in the top-left corner.
2. Select **Pause workout**.

From this menu, you’ll also see options to minimize your workout so it stays accessible at the bottom of the screen.

For example, if you realize you need to [create a custom exercise](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/328-how-to-create-a-custom-exercise) that does not exist yet, you can pause and minimize your workout, create the custom exercise, then return to your workout. The new exercise will be available to search and add when you resume the session.

### To resume a workout

1. Return to your workout.
2. Tap the three horizontal lines in the top-left corner.
3. Select **Resume workout**.
