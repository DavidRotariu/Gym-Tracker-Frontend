---

1. Go to **More** > General > **Account**.
2. Under **Security**, next to **Password**, tap **Reset**.
3. A message and button will pop up at the bottom of your screen.
4. Tap **Reset Password**. An email will be sent to the email attached to your subscription with instructions and a link to update your password.
