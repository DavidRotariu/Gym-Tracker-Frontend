You can log your weight in three different ways. 

**Option 1**: 

1. Tap the **+** button near the bottom of the screen, tap **Weight**.
2. Select the date and add your scale weight (you can also add your scale body fat estimate, if that information is available to you).
3. Tap **Save** to log your weight.

**Option 2**: 

1. Go to the **Dashboard** tab.
2. Under the **Habits** section, tap **Weigh-In**.
3. Tap on the calendar day you’d like to edit.
4. Enter your weight.
5. Tap **Save** to log your weight.

**Option 3**:

1. Go to the **Dashboard** tab.
2. Under the **Body Metrics** section, tap **Scale Weight**.
3. Tap the **+** button at the top-right corner of the screen.
4. Select the day you’d like to edit by tapping the date element in the top-middle of the editor page.
5. Enter your weight.
6. Tap **Save** to log your weight.
