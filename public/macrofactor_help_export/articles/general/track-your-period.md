1. On the main dashboard, scroll down and tap **Period** (under the General header near the bottom of the page).
2. Tap the day you'd like to edit.
3. Use the toggle beside **Are you on your period?** to indicate that you were on your period during the day you’re editing.
4. Tap **Save**.

You can enable or disable the ability to track your period by tapping **More** > **Data Visibility** > **Period Visibility**, and then toggling **Period Tracking** on or off.
