title: "Force Data Syncing (Workouts)"
section: "General"
source_url: https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/355-force-data-syncing-workouts
---

1. Ensure your data is recorded in the platform you want to sync from (Apple Health, Health Connect).
2. Make sure there’s no manual entry for the day, or a higher priority entry for the day, in MacroFactor Workouts. Higher priority entries will prevent a sync from occurring.
3. Wait 5-10 minutes.
4. Swipe down on the main dashboard (“overswiping”) until you see a little loading circle appear.
5. Once this loading circle completes, it will force an attempted sync and update your data accordingly.
