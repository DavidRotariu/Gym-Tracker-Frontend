Go to **More** > Feature Settings > **Exercises**

This page takes you to the full exercise list. At the bottom of the screen, there is a search bar where you can search for exercises and view their settings.

**Note:** When [creating](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/285-create-a-new-program-via-smart-generation) or [updating a workout or program](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/292-add-or-remove-exercises-from-a-program), you can add exercises at any time. For example, during an active workout, **tap the + button** at the end of the current exercise bar to open the exercise library. This is one of many options to add additional exercises and open the exercise search.
