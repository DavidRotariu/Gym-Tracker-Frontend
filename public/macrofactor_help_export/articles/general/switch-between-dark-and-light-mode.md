---

1. Go to the **More** tab.
2. Under the section titled **Theme**, choose between System, Light, or Dark.

"Dark" and "Light" modes should be self-explanatory. "System" mode keeps the app in the same mode as your phone: light when your phone is in light mode and dark when your phone is in dark mode.
