During a workout, you can add a note about your session, a specific exercise, or your program.

**To add a note:**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Select the workout or program you want to start.
3. Start the workout.
4. Use the action bar and scroll until you see **Note**, then tap it.
5. Choose where you want the note to apply: **Session**, **Exercise**, or **Program**.
6. Enter your note, then tap **Save**.

Your note is saved with the selected context, so you can reference it later when reviewing your workouts or program.
