Duplicate a workout if you want to make small changes without rebuilding everything from scratch. 

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Select the workout you want to duplicate.
3. Tap the three dots in the top right corner.
4. Select **Duplicate Workout**.
5. Rename the duplicated workout what you want.
6. Tap **Save and Go to Duplicate**.

This creates a separate copy of the workout that you can edit without affecting the original.
