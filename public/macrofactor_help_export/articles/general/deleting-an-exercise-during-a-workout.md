---
title: "Deleting an Exercise During a Workout"
section: "General"
source_url: https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/336-deleting-an-exercise-during-a-workout
---

1. While in your workout and viewing the exercise, use the action bar and scroll until you see **More**.
2. Tap **Remove from workout** at the bottom of the screen.
3. The exercise will be removed from the workout.
