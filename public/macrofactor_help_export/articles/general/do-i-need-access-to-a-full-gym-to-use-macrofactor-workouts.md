---

No, you do not need access to a full gym to use MacroFactor Workouts.

During setup, you select the equipment you have access to by choosing (or creating) a gym profile. MacroFactor Workouts then uses that information to build programs and recommend exercises that fit your available equipment.

You can use the app with a home gym, limited equipment, or a commercial gym. If your equipment access changes, [you can switch gym profiles or update your profiles](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/300-set-up-your-gym-profiles) to show the additions or changes over time.
