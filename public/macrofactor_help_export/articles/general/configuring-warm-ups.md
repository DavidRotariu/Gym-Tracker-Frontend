Smart warm-ups can help improve performance by manually calculating warm-up weights and reps for your session. They also help ease you into heavier sets without overdoing it. The app achieves this by automatically scaling warm-up sets based on the exercise and your current working loads.   
  
Warm-ups will be automatically added to each exercise in your workouts based on the scheme. So, by default, they are turned on.

## Enabling Smart Warm-ups

1. Go to **More** > Feature Settings > **Workouts**

2. Under ‘General’, tap on **Warm-Up**

   ![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/enzw6T3zZTvVv78bklarJvSefzTTieuLjSkHsbcflIx1Gikd6Vxbdmq1UqM2Y1nOc1EnF3R8ofn.png)
3. Under ‘Behavior’, tap **Edit** next to **Warm-Up Automation**.

   ![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/kQImTkzfLPFGHTkNGj78kz2fJ4H0lgaZAureQ2FkXPGi53z0lrhqWXliMXtHGr4QPPXP4aziiDm.png)
4. Select **Smart Warm-Up** if you want warm-up sets to be automatically added based on the weight you’re lifting.

   ![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/7Xw8J0ktbov323COD7pg4Ky7LGXf98ExbadJtjsadpEthaWbKwrkmvdDsAiJH5o4TQwWRFGH6Ww.png)
5. Your warm-ups will now be automatically added to exercises in your workouts.

**Option 2: During a workout**

1. While in an active workout, tap the three horizontal lines in the top-left corner of the screen.

   ![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/9WpXlBgnGzfPHIzg3KO5gvt1TFOpgklPGY2SOx3r1nPaDAgsXm06NCe0MiPwzUdF6r20Unc4POx.png)
2. Select **Workout Settings**.
3. Under **Warm-Up**, toggle **Add Smart Warm-Ups** on or off.

**Option 3: Add a Warm-Up set during a workout**

1. During a workout, tap the exercise you want to add a warm-up to from the top navigation bar.
2. In the action bar, under the exercise title, tap **Warm Up**.

   ![](https://staticfiles.gleap.io/gattachments/w89LRiOtXZ5zH3Km8JYRnlJGaSXiwzxtFqHZkFE2G1JVEbJlB81TiPQLOFf41XGBRyfV4YVeT0s.png)

4. Swipe left on the warm-up set, then tap the red button to remove it.

## Warm-Up Schemes

The **Warm-Up Scheme** feature lets you configure the default warm-up scheme used by the workout warm-up calculator. To get there, go to **More > Workout > Warm-Up > Warm-Up Scheme**.

## Exercise-specific warm-up schemes

You also have the option to create warm-up schemes for specific exercises. These will take precedence over your default warm-up scheme settings.

### To add a specific exercise warm-up scheme

1. While in the **Warm-Up Scheme** menu, tap **Add** next to **Add Exercise Scheme**
2. Search for and select the exercise you’d like to configure
3. Enter the warm-up scheme values, then tap **Done** to save

Warm-up schemes can be customized based on percentages, reps, and reference sets. For example, you might set one warm-up at 40% of your target set, another at 60%, and a final warm-up at 80% for 3 reps before your working sets begin.

## Configure warm-up schemes during an active workout

You can also adjust the warm-up scheme for an exercise while you are in a workout.

1. During a workout, tap the exercise you want to adjust from the top navigation bar
2. In the action bar under the exercise title, tap **Warm Up**
3. Select the **Scheme** toggle
4. Make adjustments to the warm-up scheme, then tap the **X** icon when you’re done

**Note:** Changes made here update the warm-up scheme for that exercise going forward.

## Removing warm-up sets from a workout

You can remove individual warm-up sets from the workout at any time.

**Note:** You can remove individual warm-up sets from the workout at any time by tapping the remove button in the warm-up menu, as shown below. Removing a warm-up set does not change your saved warm-up scheme.

## Hide completed sets

You can choose whether completed warm-up sets remain visible in your workout or are hidden behind the **Warm-Up** menu.

Turn **Hide Completed Sets** on or off under:  
**More > Workout > Warm-Up**
