You can manage your subscription and renewal settings through your phone’s app store or within MacroFactor.

If you want to change your subscription to a different plan, you can also do this in your phone's app store, or go to **More** > General > **Subscription** in MacroFactor Workouts, select the subscription you’d like to swap to, and then tap **Subscribe**.  
  
While on the Subscriptions page in MacroFactor, you can also view your active subscription and its renewal date. Payment details and subscription cancellations must be managed directly through your phone’s app store.  
  
[Here are the instructions](https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT202039) for canceling or changing your subscription on the Apple App Store.  
[Here are the instructions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7018481) for canceling your subscription through the Google Play Store.

If you want to read about MacroFactor and subscription bundles, [read here](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/393-how-macrofactor-subscriptions-and-bundles-work).
