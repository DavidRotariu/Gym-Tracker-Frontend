If you cannot complete a scheduled workout, you can skip it without removing it from your program.

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Select your current program and expand it using the right arrow.
3. Select the workout you want to skip, for example, **Workout C**.
4. **Tap the three dots** in the top right corner.
5. Select **Skip Workout**.

You can also:

* Un-skip the workout later if you decide to complete it by following the same process.
* [Move the workout](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/296-reorder-exercises-in-a-program) to a different day.
