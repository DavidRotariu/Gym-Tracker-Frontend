MacroFactor Workouts and MacroFactor Nutrition can each be subscribed to individually, or together as a bundled plan.

If you want to subscribe to **one app only** (either MacroFactor Nutrition or MacroFactor Workouts), the pricing options are:

* $11.99/month
* $47.99/half year
* $71.99/year

All prices listed on this page are in USD. Prices may vary based on your location’s exchange rate and tax requirements.

If you want access to **both apps**, you can subscribe to the **MacroFactor bundle**, which includes full access to both MacroFactor Nutrition and MacroFactor Workouts.

## **Option #1: Legacy MacroFactor Nutrition subscribers**

If you had an active MacroFactor Nutrition subscription before January 1, 2026 and continue to maintain your subscription, **you’ll receive free access to MacroFactor Workouts** through January 12, 2027.

After the promotional period, you will still be able to get an annual subscription for an MF bundle. Your bundled plan will be $89.99/year for access to both apps. This is a massive value: Upgrading to the bundle is only an additional $18/year (or $1.50/month) than subscribing to the yearly plan for just one app.

### **How to redeem your access:**

Step 1: Make sure you have a MacroFactor account. Check the email you used to sign in by going to More > Account > Profile > Email.  
Step 2: Download MacroFactor Workouts in the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/macrofactor-workouts-tracker/id6737156524) or [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.sbs.train).  
Step 3: Sign in with the same account you use for MacroFactor (nutrition).

If you’re eligible for promotional access, you will not be asked to pay. If you see a paywall, log out and back in using the same email and authentication method.

## **Option #2: If you’re a new MacroFactor user**

If you started using MacroFactor on or after January 1, 2026, you can subscribe to the MacroFactor bundle for $89.99/year.

The bundle includes full access to both MacroFactor Nutrition and MacroFactor Workouts.

### **If you started with MacroFactor Nutrition**

How to subscribe to the bundle:

Step 1: Open the app and go to More > Subscription.  
Step 2: Select the Bundle option and tap Subscribe.  
**Note: Make sure both apps are downloaded and sign in using the same account credentials**.

### **If you started with MacroFactor Workouts**

How to subscribe to the bundle:

Step 1: Open the app and go to More > Subscription.  
Step 2: Select the Bundle option and tap Subscribe.  
**Note: Make sure both apps are downloaded and sign in using the same account credentials**.
