---
title: "Can I Edit or Delete Logged Sets After a Workout?"
section: "General"
source_url: https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/380-can-i-edit-or-delete-logged-sets-after-a-workout
---

Yes, you can edit or delete logged sets after a workout.

**To edit a set**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Under **More**, tap **Workout History**.
3. Scroll to the workout you want to edit, tap it.
4. Under **Workout Details,** next to **Edit Workout**, tap **Edit**.
5. Make any changes needed.
6. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your changes.
7. You can also edit the training duration from this screen.

**To delete a set**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Under **More**, tap **Workout History**.
3. Scroll to the workout with the set you want to delete, tap it.
4. Under **Workout Details,** next to **Edit Workout**, tap **Edit**.
5. Swipe left on the set you want to delete and **tap the remove icon** on the right.
6. This will delete the set instantly.
7. Tap the checkmark in the top-right corner to save your changes.
