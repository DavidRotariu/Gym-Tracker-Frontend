---
title: "How to Prevent Exercises From Being Suggested"
section: "General"
source_url: https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/337-how-to-prevent-exercises-from-being-suggested
---

1. Go to **More** > Feature Settings > **Exercises**.
2. Select the exercise you don’t want to see recommended.
3. Toggle **Do Not Recommend** to *on*.

Once enabled, that specific exercise will no longer be recommended when creating new programs.
