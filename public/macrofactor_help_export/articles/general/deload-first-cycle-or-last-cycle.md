Deloads reduce training stress by temporarily lowering intensity or volume. Some people use a deload at the start of a training block to ease into new workloads and limit soreness. Others prefer a deload at the end of a block to promote recovery and feel fresher going into the next phase.

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Tap your active program.
3. Tap the **program settings icon** in the top-right corner.
4. Scroll down to **Deload** and tap **Edit**.
5. Select one of the following options:

**None:** Train continuously without scheduled reductions in intensity or volume.

**First Cycle:** Start each training block with a lower-intensity cycle to ease into new workloads and reduce soreness.

**Last Cycle**: Finish each training block with a lighter cycle to promote recovery and readiness for the next phase.

6. Tap the **X** to return to your workout program and save your selection.
