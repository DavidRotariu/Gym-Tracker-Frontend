Archiving a workout removes it from your active workout library without deleting it. This is useful if you are rotating training blocks or [cleaning up your library sections](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/322-customizing-workout-sections) while keeping workouts you may want to use again later.

**To archive a workout:**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Swipe left on the workout you want to archive.
3. Tap the **folder icon** to archive the workout
4. The workout will be removed from your current workout library and moved to your archive.

**To restore a workout:**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Under **More**, tap **Archive**.
3. Swipe left on the workout you want added back to your workout library.
4. Tap the **folder icon** to unarchive the program.
5. The program will return to your workout library.
