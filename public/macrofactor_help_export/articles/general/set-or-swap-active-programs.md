Sometimes you may want to switch from your current program to a new program you have created, or to a previously used program you want to activate again.

**Option 1: Setting a New Program as Your Active Program**

1. Tap the **+** button in the center of the bottom navigation.
2. Tap to **New Program**.
3. Complete the program setup flow.
4. At the end of the flow, tap **Activate Program**.

**Option 2: Setting a Program From Your Workout Library as Your Active Program**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Under **Workout Library**, tap **Expand** to view your full library.
3. **Drag the program** you want to use into your active program area.

After activating a new program, check your dashboard to confirm the change. You will see updated targets.

**Note:** Switching active programs does not reset or delete any previously logged workouts, muscle group data, or exercise history.
