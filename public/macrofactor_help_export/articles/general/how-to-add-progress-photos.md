### To add progress photos:

1. Go to the **Dashboard** tab.
2. Under **Body Metrics**, tap **See All** to view your inner dashboard and then tap the **Progress Photo** tile.
3. Tap the **+** button in the top-right corner of the screen.
4. Optionally, select the date on which you took progress photos, if you’re not adding photos you took on the present day.

   1. Tap the date in the top-middle of your screen.
   2. Use the calendar to navigate to the date for which you’d like to upload a progress photo.
   3. Tap the date on which you took your progress photo.
5. Tap on “Front Photo,” “Side Photo,” or “Back Photo” to add a progress picture

   1. Upload a picture from your phone’s memory or
   2. Tap “Take Photo” to launch your phone’s camera to snap a picture.
6. Crop your photo. You can zoom in or out, or rotate your photo during this step. **Tap the checkmark** in the top-right corner to upload your cropped photo.
7. Add more photos if desired, repeating steps 4 and 5. You can upload one progress photo of the front, side, and back of your body each day you add progress photos.
8. **Tap the checkmark** in the top-right corner of the screen to save your entries.

Progress photos can be useful for giving some context in physical body changes in relation to training progress over time, especially when changes in strength or volume don’t always align with scale changes alone. If you’d like some tips for taking good progress photos, you’ll likely enjoy the knowledge base entry linked above. And, if you’d like to change which progress pictures you track, check out this knowledge base entry.
