When you use the [Smart Generation](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/285-create-a-new-program-via-smart-generation) in MacroFactor Workouts, the app builds your plan using the information you provide during setup.

The program generator uses:

* Your primary training goal, such as strength or hypertrophy.
* Your training experience, which helps guide appropriate starting volumes and intensity ranges.
* Your training schedule, including how many days per week you train and how long each session is.
* Your available equipment, based on your selected gym profile.
* [Exercise exclusions](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/337-how-to-prevent-exercises-from-being-suggested), to avoid movements you do not want to perform.
* Program structure choices, such as splits and areas of emphasis.

Using these inputs, MacroFactor Workouts generates a complete program that includes exercise selection and set and rep targets that fit your setup. After you start training, your logged sets are used to improve recommendations over time.
