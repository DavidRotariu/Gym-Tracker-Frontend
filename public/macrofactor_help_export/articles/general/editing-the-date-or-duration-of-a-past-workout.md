If you logged a workout on the wrong date or want to [manually backfill workouts](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/384-can-i-backfill-past-workouts-to-have-training-history), you can edit both the workout date and duration.

**Change the workout date**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Under **More**, tap **Workout History**.
3. Scroll to the workout you want to edit and tap it.
4. Under **Workout Details**, next to **Start Time,** tap **Edit**.
5. Choose the correct date and time, then tap **Save**.

**Change the workout duration**

1. Go to the **Workout** tab.
2. Under **More**, tap **Workout History**.
3. Scroll to the workout you want to edit and tap it.
4. Under **Workout Details**, next to **Duration,** tap **Edit**.
5. Update the workout length, then tap **Save**.
