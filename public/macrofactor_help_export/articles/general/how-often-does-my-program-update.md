---
title: "How Often Does My Program Update?"
section: "General"
source_url: https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/369-how-often-does-my-program-update
---

Once a program is created, it stays the same unless you choose to edit or create a new one. This includes exercise selection, weekly layout, and sets and reps. Your program and overall structure will not change automatically. However, as you log workouts, MacroFactor Workouts *uses your training data to update recommendations*, such as suggested weights or reps. These updates occur as soon as you finish a workout (for example, when you complete Workout A this week, your targets for Workout A next will will be updated).

Changes to exercise selection or the overall structure of the program itself only occur if you manually adjust the program or create a new one altogether.
