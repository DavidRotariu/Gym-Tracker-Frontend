You can create and share before-and-after photos from progress photos and body measurements you’ve already logged in MacroFactor Workouts.

1. Go to the **Dashboard** tab.
2. Under **Body Metrics**, tap **See All** to open your inner dashboard, then tap the **Progress Photos** tile.
3. At the top of the page, tap **Open Gallery**. This opens the before-and-after photo builder.
4. Tap the button in the bottom-center of the screen. It appears as two stacked rectangles.
5. At the top of the screen, tap **Front**, **Side**, or **Back** to choose the view you want to compare.
6. Select the photos you want to use for your before-and-after comparison.

   * To select a “before” photo, first tap the top picture. Then, tap the picture in your gallery (the ribbon of photos arranged by date along the bottom of the screen) that you’d like to use as your “before” photo.
   * To select an “after” photo, first tap the bottom picture. Then, tap the picture in your gallery that you’d like to use as your “after” photo.
7. Optional: Tap the eyedropper icon in the bottom-right corner to switch between a plain white background, a dark gray background, or a background that matches the color tones of your progress photos.
8. To share your comparison, tap the share icon in the bottom-left corner to open your phone’s sharing menu. From here, you can send the images to a trainer, coach, or friend, or share them on social media.

If you’d like some tips for taking good progress photos, you’ll likely enjoy the knowledge base entry linked above.
