You can add steps to your Dashboard in the following ways:

1. Go to the **Dashboard** tab.
2. Under **General**, tap **Steps**.
3. Tap the **+** button in the top-right corner of the screen.
4. Select the date and enter your step count.
5. Tap **Save** to save your changes.

Alternatively, you can add steps using one of our supported integrations.

You can view your step data over time in the graph on this page and switch between predefined date ranges. Additional history is available below the graph in the section showing past dates.
