If you cannot perform an exercise or do not have the required equipment, you can [swap the exercise](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/299-swap-exercises) for a suitable alternative. You can make these changes during program editing or while logging a workout.

After you swap an exercise, [progression](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/305-understanding-and-using-smart-progressions) for the new exercise is based on the sets you log for it going forward. This is especially important if you were tracking data for a specific exercise. Once you swap that exercise, new data will no longer be added for it, though you can still view related context, such as moving from a barbell bench press to a dumbbell bench press.

If your equipment access changes, you can also [update or switch gym profiles](https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/300-set-up-your-gym-profiles) so future programs and recommendations better match what you have available.
