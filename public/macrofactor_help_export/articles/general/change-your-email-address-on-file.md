---
title: "Change Your Email Address on File"
section: "General"
source_url: https://help.macrofactorapp.com/en/articles/361-change-your-email-address-on-file
---

1. Go to **More** > General > **Account**.
2. Under **Security,** next to **Email**, tap **Edit**.
3. Enter your new email address, then tap **Change Email**.
4. You will receive a verification email at the new address. Open it and follow the instructions to confirm the change.
